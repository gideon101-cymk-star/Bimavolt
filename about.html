About BimaVolt

Insurance should understand the work behind the ride.

We are building rider protection around the realities of electric two-wheel transport: battery health, daily swaps and the income that depends on both.

The reason we started

A battery failure can become an income crisis.

For an electric boda boda rider, the battery is more than a component. It is an essential working asset. Premature battery wear can mean a replacement bill, time away from the road and pressure on the money a household relies on.

Most motor policies are not designed to respond to battery capacity wear. BimaVolt is being developed to address that gap with cover linked to the health of the pack and a payment approach that can fit into the swap routine.

Our aim is simple: make the cover understandable, make the claim path clear, and keep riders involved in how the product works.

What guides us

Useful protection starts with listening.

01 / CLARITY

Plain terms

Riders should know what a policy covers, what it excludes, how much it costs and what evidence a claim needs.

02 / FAIRNESS

Pricing with context

Where the right data is available, battery and riding signals can help inform pricing instead of assuming every rider has the same risk.

03 / PRACTICALITY

Designed for the day

Joining, paying and asking for help should fit around the station, the work and the rider's available time.

Why battery health matters so much

In a battery-powered boda boda business, the pack is not a side detail; it is the main operating asset. Heat, fast charging, depth of discharge, route intensity and high daily utilisation all influence degradation. Over time, that can reduce range and trigger replacement needs that are far more costly than a simple repair.

That is why BimaVolt focuses on battery health and usable performance, rather than treating the bike as if it were a traditional fuel motorcycle with a single fixed maintenance schedule.
