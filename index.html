How the system works

A better product starts with understanding the real operating model.

Insurance basics

How coverage is designed

Insurance works by pooling risk across many riders. In an electric boda boda product, that risk is tied not only to the bike but also to the battery, because the battery is the part that drives range, uptime and replacement cost.

  • Premiums can reflect battery condition, use patterns and the rider’s operating environment.
  • Claims are triggered only when a policy condition is met and the evidence supports it.
  • Clear exclusions help riders know what is and is not covered before they pay.
Electric bodas

How the bike earns money

Electric boda bodas are built for daily work: short trips, high utilisation and frequent battery swapping. They often travel long hours, charging and swapping throughout the week rather than depending on a single fuel refill model.

  • The battery is a mobile asset, so its health affects trip range and rider income.
  • Load, heat, fast charging and depth-of-discharge all affect long-term pack performance.
  • Frequent battery swaps can make a very practical pricing and support model possible.
Rider risks

What can disrupt work

When a battery degrades too quickly, riders can face a large replacement bill, time off the road and lost earnings all at once. A strong protection product helps spread that risk without making the process confusing.

  • Rapid heat build-up and repeated fast charging can reduce usable battery life.
  • High daily mileage and aggressive riding can increase wear on the pack.
  • Early claim support matters if the rider's livelihood depends on daily movement.

Good questions

Before you hit the road.

Insurance should be easy to understand before you sign up.

What does BimaVolt cover?

The product is designed around premature battery degradation and related eligible replacement claims. Final benefits, exclusions and eligibility are defined in the policy terms shown before enrolment.

How is the premium paid?

The intended model is a small contribution alongside a battery swap. The actual amount and payment arrangements depend on your plan and will be presented before you agree to cover.

Does a battery below 70% automatically mean a claim is approved?

No. Battery health can be a policy trigger, but a claim must also meet the policy's terms, eligibility requirements and verification process.

Can I sign up if I do not have a telematics-enabled bike?

Registration lets you share your details and bike information. Availability and any telemetry requirements depend on the cover offered in your area.

What happens if I change my battery or ride more?

Rider data, pack health and daily usage are part of how the product is reviewed. If your battery or usage changes materially, the product may re-evaluate the premium or policy cover accordingly.

How fast do support teams respond?

We aim to acknowledge rider issues efficiently and provide claim guidance in a short review window. Exact response times depend on the support channel and the information a rider provides.
