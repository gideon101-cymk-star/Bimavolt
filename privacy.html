Privacy

Your information deserves care.

This page explains the information the BimaVolt prototype asks for and how it is intended to support rider cover. We will publish the final legal policy before any commercial launch.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Information we may ask for

Registration may ask for your name, phone number, county, bike and battery identifiers, and a password. If you enrol in a telematics-enabled product, ride and battery measurements may also be processed to assess battery condition, calculate a price or support a claim.

Why it is used

Information is used to create and manage a rider account, explain available cover, communicate with you, assess eligibility, administer claims, and help prevent fraud or improve product safety. We aim to collect only information relevant to those purposes.

How information may be shared

Where needed to provide a policy or service, information may be shared with an insurer, battery or fleet partner, swap-station operator, payment provider, or service provider. We do not claim that information is never shared; any sharing should be limited to the service being provided and explained in the final policy.

Rider choices and requests

You may request access to or correction of your account information, ask questions about its use, or raise a concern by contacting the BimaVolt team. Applicable data-protection rights and response processes will be set out in the final policy.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the system in use. Information should be kept only as long as needed for the purpose, policy administration, or legal obligations. The registration service in this demonstration stores accounts in process memory and is not suitable for real rider information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@bimavolt.co.ke. This is a demonstration contact address and must be confirmed before public launch.

Important: This draft is informational, not legal advice. It must be reviewed against BimaVolt's actual service providers, retention rules, data-protection practices and applicable Kenyan law before use with real customers.
