Our team

Good cover takes more than a policy.

It takes people who understand insurance, mobility, battery systems and the everyday work of a rider.

Different expertise. One purpose.

Close to the people and technology this cover is meant to serve.

Building straightforward digital tools and responsive support for the questions riders have before and after they join.
