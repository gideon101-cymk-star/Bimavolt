02 / Multiple decrement risk

Separate the three ways an asset can leave the fleet.

Enter calibrated cause-specific forces per 1,000 operating cycles and an exposure period. The model assumes constant forces during that period and treats causes as competing first events.

Calibration issue to resolve before production

The printed BSM formula is an exponential of non-negative terms, so it cannot produce a BSM below 1.00. The benchmark reports 0.88 for the safe-operator segment. The benchmark premiums also cannot be independently reproduced from the document because the calibrated credibility, base premium and collision factors are not provided. Treat the reported segment figures as source benchmarks and reconcile the model basis before using it for underwriting.

Scope and assumptions

A transparent prototype, not a live underwriting decision.

What is implemented

The blueprint's BSM and credibility equations, its pay-as-you-swap premium structure, a constant-force competing-risk probability calculation, and transparent UPR/IBNR monitoring estimates.

What still needs calibration

Cause-specific decrement rates, the fleet and rider variance basis, exposure units, a collision-factor model, and approved reserve methods must be supported by validated portfolio experience and actuarial review.

Prototype boundary

This workbench runs in the browser and does not change rider account quotes, collect telemetry, trigger M-Pesa payments, or submit data to the server. Do not use its illustrative results for a policy, claim, or solvency filing.
